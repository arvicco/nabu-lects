# News

## v1.7.0 — the autumn arc *(13 October 2026)*

Eleven waves cut as one release, the registry's largest growth span.
The genealogy gained its first **American nodes** (Zapotec with the
Colonial Valley stage, Tupinambá, Guarani with the Old Guaraní of the
Jesuit reductions), the **Indic package** (Telugu, Kannada with its
Old and Middle stages, Ardhamāgadhī as the first individually-minted
Prakrit), the **Southeast Asia and Gujarat package** (Balinese and
Sasak, Malay with a Classical stage for the Jawi manuscript
tradition, Gujarati, the Tai family with Thai), the **Punjabi
package** (the granthic Old Punjabi stage for the Gurmukhī
scriptural canon, and Gurmukhi itself in the scripts table), the
**Slavic branches** with the Church Slavonic recension varieties,
and — the smallest and rarest — **Tumshuqese**, whose standing
no-consumer hold lifted when two London leaves in a Tocharian
manuscript chain were identified from Maue's handlist. Beneath the
anchors: the comparative-etymology and StarLing branch-base wings,
the dictionary-group wave, the Japanese classical ladder, and the
codemap's growth to 155 consumer-driven mappings.

## v1.6.2 — the Elamite anchor *(26 September 2026)*

Elamite as a parentless isolate (the Elamo-Dravidian hypothesis
noted as unproven), with the four-stage ladder its consuming
corpus's period vocabulary materializes: Old, Middle, Neo- and
Achaemenid Elamite.

## v1.2.0 — the Early Modern English stage *(26 August 2026)*

One additive stage: `en:early` (1500–1700), picking up exactly where
the Middle English band ends. Its first consumer staged the
twenty-five thousand texts of EEBO-TCP Phase I by imprint-year
inference.

## v1.1.0 — the Norwegian and Danish descent ladders *(24 August 2026)*

The first minor release under the v1.0.0 contract — purely additive:
Norwegian and Danish anchors with the gammelnorsk stage, letting a
consumer's Old Norse manuscript archive state its modern-code claims
honestly.

## v1.0.0 — the registry stands *(23 August 2026)*

The first tagged release: 110 anchors, 68 stages, 19 varieties, 20
scripts and 67 codemap rows — validator-checked, CI-wired, with one
live consumer resolving 148 targets at zero drift. From this tag the
identifier space is under semantic versioning: identifiers are never
renamed or re-meant, only added or deprecated.
